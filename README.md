# FUTURE_CS_02 — Phishing Email Detection & Analysis

## 📌 Internship Task

This repository contains the work completed for **Task 2: Phishing Email Detection** as part of the cybersecurity internship.

The task focuses on identifying, analysing, and classifying phishing-related email samples by examining common indicators of malicious or fraudulent communication.

The analysis helps develop practical skills in **phishing detection, email security awareness, social engineering identification, and cybersecurity risk assessment**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this task are:

1. Identify common phishing characteristics present in different email samples.
2. Analyse suspicious email content and identify potential indicators of phishing.
3. Examine sender information, branding, links, requests, and social-engineering techniques.
4. Classify email samples according to their apparent risk level.
5. Assess the potential risks associated with interacting with suspicious emails.
6. Recommend appropriate security actions for users receiving suspicious emails.
7. Improve awareness of common phishing and social-engineering techniques.
8. Develop practical skills for identifying and avoiding phishing attacks.

---

## 🔍 Task Overview

Four different email samples were examined as part of this task.

Each sample was analysed for indicators such as:

* Suspicious or unusual sender information
* Impersonation of trusted organizations
* Unexpected requests
* Suspicious links or URLs
* Urgency or pressure tactics
* Requests for personal or sensitive information
* Payment-related requests
* Unusual branding or formatting
* Social-engineering techniques
* Potential security risks

The purpose of the analysis was to determine whether an email appeared to be **legitimate, suspicious, or phishing-related** and to identify the reasoning behind the classification.

---

## 🧪 Methodology

The phishing email analysis followed a structured process:

### 1. Email Examination

Each email sample was reviewed to understand its content, sender information, purpose, and overall appearance.

### 2. Indicator Identification

Potential phishing indicators were identified, including:

* Suspicious sender addresses
* Unexpected messages
* Fake or misleading branding
* Suspicious links
* Urgent requests
* Requests for personal information
* Payment requests
* Social-engineering techniques

### 3. Risk Assessment

The identified indicators were evaluated to determine the potential risk associated with each email.

### 4. Classification

Each sample was classified based on the observed characteristics and level of risk.

### 5. Recommended Action

Appropriate actions were identified for users, such as avoiding suspicious links, not providing sensitive information, and reporting suspicious messages.

## 🛡️ Key Phishing Indicators

The following indicators were considered during the analysis:

### Suspicious Sender

Attackers may use email addresses that imitate legitimate organizations or contain unusual domains.

### Urgency

Phishing emails often create a sense of urgency or pressure users into taking immediate action.

### Suspicious Links

Links may redirect users to fraudulent websites designed to collect credentials or other sensitive information.

### Impersonation

Attackers may imitate trusted companies, services, or individuals to make fraudulent messages appear legitimate.

### Requests for Information

Unexpected requests for passwords, account information, payment details, or other sensitive information can indicate phishing.

### Social Engineering

Phishing attacks commonly manipulate users through techniques involving fear, urgency, curiosity, rewards, or authority.

---

## ⚠️ Security Recommendations

Users should follow these practices when dealing with suspicious emails:

* Do not click suspicious links or attachments.
* Verify the sender before responding.
* Check the actual domain of links and email addresses.
* Never provide passwords or sensitive information through unexpected emails.
* Be cautious of urgent or threatening messages.
* Verify payment requests through an independent communication channel.
* Report suspicious emails to the appropriate IT or security team.
* Delete or quarantine confirmed phishing messages.
* Use multi-factor authentication for additional account protection.
* Stay aware of common social-engineering techniques.
